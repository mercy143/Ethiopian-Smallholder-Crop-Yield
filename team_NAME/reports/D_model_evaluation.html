D7 Error analysis

The principal pattern is that high-altitude and unusually hot plots are harder to predict, likely because climate extremes push the crop response outside the common range captured by the training data.

D8 Response to findings

The model was tuned on the training data and the residual analysis was used to prioritize weather features and crop-specific interactions.

D9 Plain-language metric

The final random-forest model has RMSE = 0.5377 tons/ha and MAE = 0.3899 tons/ha. Relative to the mean yield of 2.77 tons/ha, this is roughly 19.4% RMSE and 14.1% MAE.
